How to Reach

By Road

Solapur is well connected by highways to major cities with regular government and private buses[cite: 52, 53].

CityDistance
Pune250 km [cite: 55]
Hyderabad300 km [cite: 56]
Mumbai400 km [cite: 57]
Bengaluru380 km [cite: 58]

By Rail

Solapur Railway Station is a major junction connecting Mumbai, Pune, Hyderabad, Chennai, and Bengaluru[cite: 60]. Rail travel is highly convenient[cite: 61].

By Air

Solapur has a small airport with limited services[cite: 63]. Nearest major airports are Pune International and Hyderabad International[cite: 64, 65].

Local Transport

Visitors can use auto rickshaws, taxis, and city buses to move around[cite: 69, 70, 71]. Nearby pilgrimage towns like Pandharpur and Akkalkot are easily accessible by road[cite: 72].
