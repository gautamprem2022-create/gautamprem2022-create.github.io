Famous Dishes

Jowar Bhakri: Traditional flatbread made from sorghum flour, served with spicy curries and chutneys[cite: 76, 77].
Shenga Chutney: Dry peanut chutney made with roasted peanuts, garlic, and spices[cite: 78, 79].
Misal Pav: Spicy sprouted lentil curry served with bread[cite: 81, 82].
Puran Poli: Sweet flatbread filled with jaggery and lentils, prepared for special occasions[cite: 83, 84].
